Delete Your InfoApp Account

This page explains how to delete your InfoApp account and what happens to your data when you do. It is provided so that InfoApp users — whether or not they currently have the app installed — can find this information and request account deletion.

How to delete your account (in the app)

  1. Open the InfoApp app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll down to the Danger zone section.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Read the warning, check "I understand this action is permanent and cannot be undone", then tap Delete my account.
  6. Confirm by tapping Delete account in the dialog that appears.

Once confirmed, your account is deleted immediately and you are automatically signed out. This action cannot be undone and InfoApp cannot restore a deleted account.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes the following from InfoApp's backend, right away:

  • Your profile (display name, username, avatar, bio)
  • Your referral history and reward wallet balance
  • Your saved and liked articles
  • Chat messages you sent, and your chat/friend connections stored on InfoApp's backend

What is retained, and why

InfoApp's chat design keeps a copy of each message on the devices of the people in that conversation, not permanently on InfoApp's servers. This means that a message you already sent to a friend may still exist on that friend's own device after you delete your account — the same way it would with any messaging app. InfoApp's backend copy of your messages is removed as part of account deletion, but InfoApp has no way to remove a copy that already exists on someone else's device.

Aside from that, InfoApp does not retain your account data after deletion.

If you can't use the in-app option

If you are unable to sign in or otherwise can't complete deletion inside the app, you can request deletion by emailing abhilashaiitd@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account. We will process the request and confirm once it's done.

This page is provided for InfoApp users to request account deletion, and is publicly accessible without needing to sign in. For a full description of what InfoApp collects and how it is used, see the Privacy Policy inside the InfoApp app (Profile → Privacy Policy).
